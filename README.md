🎁 Gift Genie

Never forget a birthday. Never lose a gift idea.

📱 Requires iOS 27 or later

Gift Genie was built using Apple Shortcuts on iOS 27 and is not backward-compatible with earlier iOS versions.

Gift Genie is a free Apple Shortcut that tracks birthdays, saves gift ideas, and reminds you when it’s time to start shopping.

No subscriptions. No separate app. Just Apple Shortcuts, Notes, and Reminders.

✨ Features

* 🎂 Birthday Tracking: Save people and their birthdays.
* 🎁 Gift Ideas: Save gift ideas for anyone in your database.
* 🔎 Gift Lookup by Person: Select someone to see only their saved gift ideas.
* 🔔 Smart Reminders: Automatically creates reminders 30 days before birthdays.
* 📝 Apple Notes: Stores birthdays and gift ideas in one convenient note.
* 📋 Birthday Review: View saved birthdays, including upcoming ages.
* 🎁 Gift Review: Displays saved ideas in a convenient, titled popup, with a helpful message when no gifts have been saved.
* 🗑️ Manage People: Add or delete people and their information.
* 🔄 Update Checker: Check for new versions directly from the shortcut.
* 🔒 Privacy: Your birthday and gift data stays in your own Apple Notes.

📲 Installation

Download Gift Genie v3.0

1. Open the download link on your iPhone running iOS 27 or later.
2. Tap Add Shortcut.
3. Run Gift Genie and follow the prompts.

Gift Genie automatically creates an Apple Note to store your information.

🚀 How It Works

1. Add a person: Enter their name and birthday.
2. Save gift ideas: Add gift suggestions whenever inspiration strikes.
3. Review gifts by person: Choose someone to see their saved gift ideas without scrolling through your entire database.
4. Get reminded: Receive a reminder 30 days before their birthday.
5. Review birthdays: See your saved birthdays and the ages people will be turning.
6. Open your database: Access your Gift Genie Apple Note whenever you want.
